## ICP definitions (classify by what the company DOES for its customers, not by what it calls itself)

Every ICP company moves or holds other people's money and therefore runs a money-operations process
(settlement, reconciliation, payouts, exceptions, disputes, refunds, funding, withdrawals). If no such process
is visible on the company's own site, it is NOT a fit.

- **ICP-1 PSP / acquiring** — accepts payments for merchants: payment gateway, payment facilitator, acquirer,
  merchant aggregator, payment links / POS / QR acceptance. Process: merchant settlement, refunds, chargebacks.
- **ICP-2 Fintech / wallets / neobanks** — holds customer balances: e-wallet, e-money institution, digital bank,
  neobank, super-app wallet. Process: top-ups, transfers, withdrawals, failed and reversed transactions.
- **ICP-3 Cards / issuing / BaaS** — issues cards or provides banking infrastructure to other companies: card
  issuing platform, issuer processor, card programme manager, BaaS / embedded-finance provider, sponsor bank
  with a fintech programme. Process: authorisation → clearing → settlement, programme reconciliation, disputes.
- **ICP-4 Cross-border / FX / PayFi** — moves money across borders or currencies: remittance, B2B cross-border
  payments, FX / collection accounts, payout networks, stablecoin-based payment rails for businesses.
  Process: corridor payouts, FX settlement, returned and delayed transfers.
- **ICP-5 Digital assets / crypto / RWA** — operates crypto or tokenised-asset money movement for customers:
  exchange, broker, custodian, on/off-ramp, stablecoin issuer or orchestration, tokenisation / RWA platform.
  Process: fiat ↔ crypto funding, withdrawals, custody movements, chain/bank reconciliation.
- **ICP-6 Investments / wealthtech** — holds client money for investing: digital broker, robo-advisor, wealth
  app, investment / savings platform, fund distribution platform. Process: funding → order/settlement →
  withdrawal, corporate actions, cash reconciliation.
- **ICP-7 Lending / credit / BNPL** — lends money: digital lender, BNPL, credit card fintech, SME or consumer
  lending platform, earned-wage access. Process: disbursement, repayment collection, failed debits, reconciliation.

Not a fit for any ICP: pure software vendors that never touch funds (accounting software, KYC-only, fraud-only,
analytics), consultancies, media, traditional banks without a fintech or API programme, companies with no live
product.

If a company fits several, pick the segment of its PRIMARY revenue-generating money flow.
