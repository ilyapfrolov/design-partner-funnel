## ICP — по документу «HAIA — Financial Buyer Discovery ICP» (Notion)

A company fits if it is a financial company that runs at least one of the listed financial Jobs for its customers.
Size: mostly small, growing and mid-sized companies (do not reject larger ones, just prefer small–mid).

| Segment (exact name) | Company type | Financial Jobs that make it a fit |
|---|---|---|
| ICP-1 PSP / acquiring | PSPs, payment facilitators, merchant acquirers | merchant onboarding, payment operations, settlement, reconciliation, disputes, launching or changing payment programmes |
| ICP-2 Fintech / wallets / neobanks | multi-product fintechs, wallets, neobanks, business banking | accounts, cards, payouts, credit products, customer servicing and coordination of operations between them |
| ICP-3 Cards / issuing / BaaS | cards, issuing, embedded finance and BaaS platforms | launching card programmes, issuer and programme-manager operations, partner oversight, disputed transactions, servicing |
| ICP-4 Cross-border / FX / PayFi | cross-border payments, FX, stablecoin PayFi | funding, conversion, transfer, beneficiary outcome, refunds, exceptions, partner operations |
| ICP-5 Digital assets / crypto / RWA | digital asset platforms, crypto exchanges, custody, RWA | fiat on/off ramps, tokenised assets, custody, investor/client settlement and governance |
| ICP-6 Investments / wealthtech | investment platforms, wealthtech, brokers | client onboarding, subscriptions, trade settlement, withdrawals, entitlement and servicing |
| ICP-7 Lending / credit / BNPL | BNPL, embedded credit, SME finance, digital lenders | origination, decisions, disbursement, repayment, partner handoffs, servicing |

Not a fit: companies that run none of these Jobs — pure software vendors that never touch customer money flows
(accounting, KYC-only, analytics), consultancies, media, companies without a live product.
If a company fits several segments, pick the one of its main money flow.
